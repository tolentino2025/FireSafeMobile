Privacy Policy

FireSafe ITM — Inspection, Testing and Maintenance of fire protection systems

Last updated: September 18, 2026. This policy describes how FireSafe ITM collects, uses, shares and protects your data, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law No. 13,709/2018) and the policies of the Apple App Store and Google Play. This is a translation of the Portuguese version; in case of conflict, the Portuguese version prevails.

Summary: FireSafe ITM is a support tool for fire protection inspections. We collect only the data needed to run the app (account, inspection data, photos, location of the inspected site). We do not sell your data, we do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not receive your payment card details.

1. Who we are (Controller)

FireSafe ITM is operated by Jonel Incêndio, Brazil. For privacy questions and to exercise your rights, contact: suporte@firesafeitm.com.

3. How we use data

We use your data to: provide and operate the ITM service; generate and share PDF reports (when you choose to); send reminders; sync data across your devices; validate subscriptions and licenses; and comply with legal obligations. The legal bases (LGPD) are performance of the contract/service, your consent (e.g. location, notifications) and our legitimate interest in the security and improvement of the app.

4. Payments and subscriptions

When you purchase a subscription inside the app, payment is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive, see or store your credit card or bank details.

We receive from the store only what is needed to unlock access: the transaction identifier (receipt), the plan purchased and the expiration date. Apple and Google handle this data under their own privacy policies, over which we have no control.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers that make the service possible (processors):

6. Where data is stored and international transfers

Inspection data, photos and accounts are stored on your device and, when you are signed in, synced to servers located in Brazil (São Paulo region).

Some ancillary services — push notifications, transactional email and payment processing — involve international transfers of data to the United States and the European Union, under articles 33 and following of the LGPD. These transfers are limited to the minimum needed for the purpose (for example, the email address of the recipient of an invitation) and are made to providers that offer contractual data protection safeguards.

7. Security

We apply: encryption in transit (HTTPS), per-user/per-company isolation through database access rules (Row Level Security), and private file storage reachable only through signed, time-limited URLs. No system is completely immune to incidents; in the event of a relevant security incident, we will notify you and the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD) as required by the LGPD.

8. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active or as needed for the purposes above and for legal obligations. You can delete it at any time (section 10).

9. Your rights

You may request: confirmation of and access to your data; correction; anonymization or deletion; portability; information about sharing; and withdrawal of consent. To exercise these rights, contact suporte@firesafeitm.com. We will respond within 15 days. You can also revoke location and notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

10. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and personal data directly in the app, under Profile > Privacy and Terms > Delete Account, or request deletion through firesafeitm.com/excluir-conta or by email at suporte@firesafeitm.com. We complete the request within 30 days, except for data the law requires us to retain.

Deleting your account does not cancel subscriptions purchased through the App Store or Google Play — cancel them in your account settings in the respective store as well.

11. Children

FireSafe ITM is intended for professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children or adolescents.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated in the app or by email. The date at the top shows the version in force.

Notice: FireSafe ITM is a support tool and does not replace the engineer of record, professional certifications, official permits or reports, or the applicable codes and standards, and it is not an emergency service.
